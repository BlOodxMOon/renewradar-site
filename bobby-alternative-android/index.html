Why people look for Bobby on Android

Bobby is a well-liked iOS app for keeping a manual list of subscriptions. It is available only for iPhone and iPad, so Android users, and people who switch from iPhone to Android, need something else.

What RenewRadar has in common with Bobby

  • Manual entry. No bank connection; you add each subscription yourself.
  • Reminders before renewals on the device.
  • Totals so you can see what everything costs together.
  • Free to start, with a one-time upgrade rather than another subscription.

What RenewRadar adds

  • Free trials come first. A dedicated Trials section shows the days left and the price after the trial, and reminders count down to the trial end.
  • Due this week and later this month. Home groups what is coming up next.
  • Personal, work or business. Tag each subscription by who it is for.
  • Pause, cancel and restore without losing the details.
  • Pro reports and CSV export for the next 12 months of charges.

If you are moving from an iPhone, add your subscriptions from Bobby's list into RenewRadar with their next charge dates. It takes about a minute each.
