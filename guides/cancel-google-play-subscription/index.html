How to Cancel a Subscription on Google Play

Subscriptions and trials you started in an Android app are usually billed by Google Play. Here is how to cancel them in under a minute.

Updated November 14, 2026 · 4 minute read

On this page

  1. On an Android phone
  2. On the web
  3. What happens after you cancel
  4. Cannot find the subscription?
  5. Pause instead of cancel

Cancel on an Android phone or tablet

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Make sure you are signed in to the Google account you subscribed with.
  3. Tap your profile picture in the top right corner.
  4. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  5. Select the subscription you want to cancel.
  6. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the instructions to confirm.

Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription. You will keep being charged until you cancel it in Google Play.

Cancel on the web

  1. Go to play.google.com and sign in with the same Google account.
  2. Open your account menu and choose Payments & subscriptions.
  3. Open Subscriptions, choose the one to cancel and select Cancel subscription.

The web version is useful when the subscription is on a phone you no longer have.

What happens after you cancel

  • You keep access for the time you have already paid for, including the rest of a free trial.
  • You get an email from Google Play confirming the cancellation. Keep it until the end date has passed.
  • No automatic refund. Cancelling stops future charges. For a charge you did not want, use Google Play's refund request, or contact the app's developer.
  • The app's data stays with the service. Cancelling does not delete your account with that service. Delete it separately if you want to.

Cannot find the subscription?

If it is not listed in Google Play, it is billed somewhere else:

  • Another Google account. Switch accounts in the Play Store and check again.
  • The service's website. If you signed up on the web, cancel in your account there.
  • Apple. If you subscribed on an iPhone, cancel in your Apple account settings.
  • A carrier or bundle. Some plans are billed through a phone or internet provider.

Our guide to cancelling a free trial explains how to find out who bills you.

Pause instead of cancel

Some apps let you pause a Google Play subscription for a while instead of cancelling. If the option is available, you will see it in the same Subscriptions screen. Billing restarts automatically when the pause ends, so set a reminder for that date. In RenewRadar, mark the item as paused and edit the next charge date when it resumes.
