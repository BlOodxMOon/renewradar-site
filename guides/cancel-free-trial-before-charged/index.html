How to Cancel a Free Trial Before You Are Charged

Cancelling a free trial is usually quick. The tricky part is knowing who bills you and doing it in time. Here is how to get both right.

Updated November 14, 2026 · 6 minute read

On this page

  2. 2. Trials on Google Play
  3. 3. Trials on the App Store
  4. 4. Trials on a website
  5. 5. When to cancel

1. Find out who bills you

The same service can bill you in different ways depending on where you signed up. This decides where you cancel:

  • Started in an Android app and paid through Google Play: cancel in Google Play.
  • Started on an iPhone or iPad and paid through Apple: cancel in your Apple account settings.
  • Started on the service's website with a card or wallet: cancel in your account on that website.
  • Added through a phone carrier, bank or bundle: cancel with that provider.

Not sure? Search your email for the sign-up confirmation. The sender (Google Play, Apple or the service itself) tells you who bills you.

Uninstalling an app does not cancel its subscription or trial. You have to cancel it where you are billed.

2. Cancel a trial billed through Google Play

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the top right.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  4. Choose the trial and tap Cancel subscription.
  5. Follow the steps to confirm.

Google Play sends a confirmation email. In most cases you keep access until the end of the trial. Our Google Play guide covers the web version and what happens next.

3. Cancel a trial billed through the App Store

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose the trial and tap Cancel Free Trial or Cancel Subscription.

If you do not see the trial there, it is probably billed by the service directly or through Google Play.

4. Cancel a trial on a website

For services you signed up for on the web, cancellation is in your account on that website. Look for Account, Billing, Plan or Membership. A few things to watch for:

  • Several steps. Some sites show offers before the final cancel button. Keep going until you see a confirmation.
  • Notice periods. Some plans must be cancelled a set number of days before the trial ends. Check the terms when you sign up.
  • Pause is not cancel. Pausing usually restarts billing automatically later.

5. When to cancel

If you already know you will not keep it, cancel the same day you sign up. Many services, including subscriptions billed through Google Play, keep the trial running until its end date after you cancel.

If you want to decide later, set reminders for a few days before the end, not on the last day. Time zones and billing systems do not always charge at midnight in your time zone, so the last day can be too late.

RenewRadar does exactly this: add the trial with its end date and the price afterwards, and it reminds you before the trial ends. See free trial reminders.

6. Confirm the cancellation worked

  • Keep the confirmation email until the date has passed.
  • Check the subscription list where you cancelled. It should show an end date instead of a renewal date.
  • Look at your statement after the trial end date. If you were charged anyway, contact the service or the store with your confirmation. Google Play and Apple both have refund request pages.
