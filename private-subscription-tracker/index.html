Private by design

A subscription tracker that does not need your bank login.

Many money apps ask you to connect your bank so they can find subscriptions for you. RenewRadar takes the opposite approach: you tell it what you pay for, and nothing leaves your phone.

Bank-linked trackers versus manual trackers

Bank-linked apps can find subscriptions automatically by reading your transactions. In exchange, a third party gets ongoing access to your financial history, and the app usually only works with banks in certain countries.

A manual tracker asks for about a minute per subscription. In exchange, nobody else sees your spending, it works in any country and with any card or wallet, and it keeps working without internet.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored in the app's private storage on your phone: names, prices, currencies, billing cycles, dates, categories, notes and reminder settings. Reminders are local notifications created on the phone itself, not sent from a server.

There is no RenewRadar account, so there is no profile, email address or password to leak.

The only network requests the app makes

RenewRadar makes two kinds of requests, and neither contains your subscriptions:

  • A weekly service list. A plain file of popular service names, categories and colors that powers the suggestions when you type a name.
  • Purchase checks. If you buy Pro, Google Play processes the payment, and RevenueCat confirms the purchase using a random anonymous ID.

The details are in the privacy policy.

What RenewRadar does not do

  • No bank or card connections.
  • No accounts, emails or phone numbers.
  • No ads, advertising IDs or analytics.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is none to share.

Keeping a copy of your list

Because nothing is stored on a server, uninstalling the app removes your list. Pro users can export the full list as a CSV file at any time and save it wherever they like, through the share sheet. The file goes only where you send it.
