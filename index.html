Private by design

Your list stays on your phone.

You type in what you pay for, and it never leaves the device. We run no server that receives your subscriptions, so there is nothing for us to lose or sell.

How RenewRadar handles data
  • No account.Open the app and start. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No bank connection.You type in what you pay for. RenewRadar never asks for your bank login.
  • No ads, no tracking.No ad networks and no analytics, so nothing follows you around.
