How it works

Four small steps, then it watches for you.

  1. 01

    Add what you pay for

    Search for a service or type your own. Enter the price, how often you are billed and the next renewal date.

  2. 02

    Choose your reminders

    Pick how many days before each renewal you want a heads-up. Every subscription keeps its own timing.

  3. 03

    Watch the radar

    Home lists everything by next renewal, with monthly and yearly totals and a countdown on every free trial.

  4. 04

    Stay in control

    Edit, pause or mark a subscription as cancelled whenever things change. Renewal dates move forward on their own.

Any currency

Add each subscription in the currency you are billed in. With no exchange rates, you get a clear total for each currency instead of a guess.

Free trials, counted down

See the days left on every trial and the price you will pay when it ends, before it does.

Works offline

Everything runs on your phone, so reminders and totals work on a plane, on a train or in a basement.

Private by design

Your list stays on your phone.

  • No account.
  • No bank connection.
  • No ads, no tracking.

You type in what you pay for, and it never leaves the device. We run no server that receives your subscriptions, so there is nothing for us to lose or sell.
