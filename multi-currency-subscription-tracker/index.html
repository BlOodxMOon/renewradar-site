Who needs a multi-currency tracker

More people pay in several currencies than you might think:

  • People who moved abroad and still pay for services at home.
  • Freelancers and remote workers who pay for tools in US dollars while earning in another currency.
  • Anyone whose app store charges in the local currency while websites bill in dollars or euros.
  • Families who share plans across countries.

Most trackers assume a single currency, or convert everything with a rate that may not match what your bank actually charged.

How currencies work in RenewRadar

Every subscription has its own price and its own currency. When you add one, USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, PHP, INR and JPY are one tap away, and you can type any other three-letter currency code, such as SGD, MXN or BRL.

You also choose a default currency in Settings, which is filled in for new subscriptions so you do not have to pick it every time.

Prices are stored exactly as you enter them. Reminders show the amount in the original currency, so the number in the notification matches the number on your statement.

Why totals stay per currency

Exchange rates move every day, and banks and card networks add their own margins. A converted total can look precise and still be wrong by several percent.

So when your subscriptions use more than one currency, RenewRadar shows a separate monthly and yearly total for each currency instead of guessing a rate. Spend by category on the Stats screen works the same way. You always see real amounts you will actually be charged.

If all your subscriptions use one currency, you simply get one monthly and one yearly total.

Tips for tracking mixed currencies

  • Use the currency on the receipt, not the one your bank converts it into. That is the amount that will be charged again.
  • Check card fees. Some cards add a foreign transaction fee to every charge in another currency, which can make a local plan cheaper.
  • Watch app store pricing. Google Play and the App Store charge in your local currency, often at different prices from the service's website.
  • Review yearly plans. A large yearly charge in another currency is exposed to a whole year of exchange rate changes.
