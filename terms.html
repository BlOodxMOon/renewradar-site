2What the app does

RenewRadar lets you list your subscriptions and free trials by hand, see what they cost, and get reminders before renewal and trial end dates. The app does not connect to your bank, does not cancel subscriptions for you, and does not know about charges you have not entered.

Totals and renewal dates are estimates based on what you enter. They are for your own planning and are not financial advice.

  • The free version lets you track up to five active subscriptions and trials. Cancelled items do not count toward this limit.
