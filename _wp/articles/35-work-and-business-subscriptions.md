Freelancers, contractors and small business owners often pay for work tools and personal services from the same card or the same Google account. Over time, it becomes hard to answer simple questions: what does my business spend on software each month? Which of these charges can I claim?

## Why keep them apart

- **Clear business costs.** You know what your work tools cost per month and per year.
- **Easier taxes and bookkeeping.** Business subscriptions are often deductible expenses in many countries. Check with an accountant for your situation.
- **Better decisions.** Personal subscriptions are judged by enjoyment; work ones by whether they earn their cost.
- **Easier handover.** If a business tool is billed to you personally, a list makes it easier to move it to a company card.

## The three labels

RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, has a **Used for** field with three options: Personal, Work and Business. It defaults to Personal.

![Personal, Work and Business labels](fig:a)

On the Home screen, Work and Business subscriptions show a small briefcase or building badge on the service circle, so you can tell them apart at a glance.

## What counts as work or business?

- **Work:** tools you pay for yourself to do your job, such as a design app, a writing tool or a course platform you use for work.
- **Business:** subscriptions for your own company or freelance business, such as hosting, domains, accounting software and team tools.
- **Personal:** everything else.

Use whichever split makes sense for you and your accountant.

## Add the details you will need later

The notes field is useful for:

- Which card or account the subscription is billed to.
- The invoice email address or where to download receipts.
- Who in your team uses it.

## Exporting for your records

RenewRadar Pro can export your full list as a CSV file, including each subscription's label, price, currency, cycle and next date. Open it in a spreadsheet, filter by Work or Business, and you have a list of recurring business costs for your bookkeeping.

## A tip for multi-currency tools

Many software tools bill in US dollars. Enter each subscription in the currency it is billed in. RenewRadar keeps a separate total for each currency, so your numbers match your statements. See our guide to [multi-currency subscriptions](/guides/multi-currency-subscriptions/).

## The payoff

A few seconds of labelling when you add a subscription saves hours at tax time and makes it obvious which tools are worth keeping.
