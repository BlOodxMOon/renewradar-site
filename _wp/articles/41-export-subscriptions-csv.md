RenewRadar keeps your subscription list on your phone. For day-to-day use, that is ideal: it is private and works offline. But there are times when you want a copy elsewhere. RenewRadar Pro includes a CSV export for exactly that.

## What is a CSV file?

CSV stands for comma-separated values. It is a plain text format that every spreadsheet app can open: Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel, LibreOffice and Apple Numbers. Each subscription becomes a row, and each detail becomes a column.

## How to export

![Steps to export your list](fig:a)

![Export data as CSV in RenewRadar Settings](shot:settings-export "In Settings, Export data as CSV is under RenewRadar Pro.")

The file goes only where you send it. RenewRadar does not upload it anywhere by itself.

## What is included

![What the CSV includes](fig:b)

The export includes cancelled and paused subscriptions too, with their status, so you have a complete record.

## Good uses for the export

### A backup before changing phones

Because RenewRadar has no account or cloud sync, your list stays on your phone. Export a CSV before you switch phones, keep it somewhere safe, and use it to re-add your subscriptions on the new phone.

### Bookkeeping and taxes

Freelancers and small businesses can filter the CSV by the **Used for** column to list Work and Business subscriptions. Share it with your accountant or import it into your bookkeeping.

### A household budget

If you keep a budget spreadsheet, paste in the monthly cost column. It is already converted from weekly, quarterly and yearly cycles.

### Sharing with your family

Send a copy to a partner so both of you know what the household pays for.

## Tips

- **The file is named with the date**, such as renewradar-2026-12-26.csv, so you always know which copy is newest.
- **Store backups somewhere private.** The file contains your subscriptions and notes in plain text.
- **Open it in a spreadsheet app**, not a text editor, for the easiest reading.

## Is export available in the free version?

Export is part of RenewRadar Pro, along with unlimited subscriptions and detailed reports. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The free version includes reminders, trial countdowns and totals for up to five subscriptions and trials.
