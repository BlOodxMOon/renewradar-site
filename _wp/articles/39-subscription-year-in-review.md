The end of the year is a natural time to review what you spend. Subscriptions are a good place to start, because small monthly decisions add up to big yearly numbers.

You do not need a spreadsheet or an afternoon. Here is a review that takes about fifteen minutes.

![The 15-minute review](fig:a)

## 1. Look at your yearly total

Add up what you pay per year across all subscriptions. If you use RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, the yearly total is at the top of the Home screen.

Ask yourself: is this number what you expected? Higher? Lower?

## 2. Check spending by category

Which category costs the most: streaming, software, music, fitness, games? The Stats screen in RenewRadar breaks your spending down by category.

![An example year by category](fig:b)

Category totals often reveal patterns, like several services that do the same thing.

## 3. Mark what you actually used

Go through the list and note, honestly, which subscriptions you used in the last month. Be strict. "I might use it" does not count.

## 4. List what renews in January

January is a heavy month for renewals: holiday trials end, yearly plans started last January renew, and new year offers begin. Sort your list by next renewal and look at the next four weeks.

RenewRadar Pro includes a report of the charges expected over the next 12 months, which shows at a glance which months will be expensive.

## 5. Pick one change for next year

Do not try to fix everything. Choose one change, such as:

- Cancel the subscription you used least this year.
- Switch your most-used service from monthly to yearly.
- Rotate two streaming services instead of keeping both.
- Turn on reminders for every yearly plan.

## Celebrate the good ones

A review is not only about cutting. Notice the subscriptions that gave you real value this year: the music you listened to every day, the app that helped you work. Those are worth paying for.

## Make next year's review easier

If you track subscriptions as you go, next year's review takes five minutes instead of fifteen. Add each new subscription and trial the day you start it, mark cancellations as you make them, and let reminders handle the dates in between.
