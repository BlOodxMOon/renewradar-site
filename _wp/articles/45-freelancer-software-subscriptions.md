Freelancers often need a stack of tools: something to design, something to write, somewhere to host work, somewhere to store files, and something to send invoices. Each one is a subscription, and many are billed yearly in US dollars.

![Common freelancer subscriptions](fig:a)

## Why freelance tool costs creep up

- **Project-based sign-ups.** A client needs a specific tool, you subscribe, the project ends, the subscription does not.
- **Yearly plans.** Cheaper per month, but easy to forget until they renew.
- **Currency differences.** Tools billed in dollars cost more when your currency weakens, plus card fees.
- **Overlapping tools.** Over time, you may pay for two tools that do the same job.

## A simple routine

![A freelancer's subscription routine](fig:b)

### Label work and business tools

In RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, each subscription has a **Used for** label: Personal, Work or Business. Work and Business items show a small badge on Home, so your tool stack is easy to see next to your personal subscriptions.

### Enter the real currency

Enter each tool in the currency you are billed in. RenewRadar keeps a separate monthly and yearly total for each currency, so your numbers match your statements without guessed exchange rates.

### Early reminders for yearly plans

Set yearly tools to remind you 14 days and 3 days before renewal. That gives you time to check whether you still need the tool, whether a cheaper plan fits, or whether you can switch to monthly during quiet periods.

### Review after projects

When a big project ends, look at the tools you added for it. Cancel what you no longer need, and mark it as cancelled in the app.

### Export for bookkeeping

RenewRadar Pro exports your full list as a CSV, including the Used for label, price, currency and cycle. Filter by Work and Business for your accountant or your expense records.

## Tip: note the billing details

Use the notes field for the card used, the account email and where to download invoices. When tax time comes, you know where every receipt lives.

## Keep the tools that pay for themselves

The goal is not the smallest tool stack. It is a stack where every subscription earns its place, and nothing renews without you deciding it should.
