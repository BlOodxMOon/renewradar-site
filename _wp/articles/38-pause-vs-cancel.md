When you go to cancel a subscription, many services offer an alternative: pause it for one, two or three months. Gyms, meal kits, streaming services and Google Play subscriptions all use pauses. It sounds harmless, but it works differently from cancelling.

## What pausing usually means

- **No charges during the pause.**
- **Your account, preferences and history are kept.**
- **Billing restarts automatically** at the end of the pause, usually without another confirmation.

That last point is the catch. A pause is like a free trial in reverse: doing nothing at the end means paying again.

![Pause and cancel compared](fig:a)

## When to pause

- You know you will be back soon, for example after a trip or a busy month.
- The service has a discount or old price you would lose by cancelling.
- Your data or progress would be lost if you cancelled.

## When to cancel

- You are not sure you will come back.
- You are rotating services and will return only when something new appears.
- The pause would end at an inconvenient time, such as just before a holiday.

Remember that cancelling usually keeps your access until the end of the period you paid for, and many services keep your account so you can return later.

## Tracking a pause

A pause needs a reminder before it ends, just like a trial. Otherwise, billing restarts quietly.

RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, has a **Pause** button on each subscription's detail screen. A paused subscription stays on Home, dimmed, with no upcoming reminders and no effect on your totals. When the service starts billing again, tap **Resume** and set the next renewal date.

Because a paused item in RenewRadar has no reminders, add a note with the date the service's pause ends. Or keep it active with the restart date as the next renewal, so you get a reminder before billing resumes.

![Pause and cancel in RenewRadar](fig:b)

## Tracking a cancellation

When you cancel, tap **Mark as cancelled**. The subscription moves to the Cancelled list at the bottom of Home. It stops counting in your totals and toward the free limit of five. If you come back later, open it and tap **Restore**.

## The simple rule

Pause when you know when you are coming back. Cancel when you do not. Either way, make sure something reminds you before the charges start again.
