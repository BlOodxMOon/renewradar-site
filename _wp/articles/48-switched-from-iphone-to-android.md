Moving from iPhone to Android is mostly about contacts, photos and apps. Subscriptions are easy to forget, and they are the part that keeps costing money.

## Apple subscriptions do not move or stop

Subscriptions bought through the App Store are billed by Apple. They continue after you stop using your iPhone, even if you never open the app again. They do not appear in Google Play on your new phone.

The same is true in reverse: subscriptions billed through Google Play will not show up in Apple's settings.

## Find your Apple subscriptions

You can see them:

- On your old iPhone or iPad: **Settings**, your name, **Subscriptions**.
- On the web: sign in to your Apple account and look for **Subscriptions**.
- In your email: search for receipts from Apple.

## Decide what to do with each one

![Steps when switching](fig:a)

- **Keep using the service on Android?** Many services work across platforms. You can keep the Apple subscription and sign in on Android, but managing it requires your Apple account. Many people prefer to cancel the Apple subscription and subscribe again through Google Play or the service's website.
- **Do not need it any more?** Cancel it in your Apple account.
- **iCloud storage?** If you move your photos and files to another service, remember to cancel or downgrade the iCloud plan.

Cancel the old subscription before the new one starts, so you do not pay for both in the same month.
