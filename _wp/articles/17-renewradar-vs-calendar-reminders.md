A popular tip for avoiding surprise subscription charges is to put a reminder in your calendar the day you sign up. It works, up to a point. Many people who try it end up with a calendar full of repeating events and still no clear picture of what they pay.

## What calendar reminders do well

- They are already on your phone.
- Repeating events can follow a monthly or yearly cycle.
- They work offline once synced.

For one or two free trials, a calendar event is perfectly fine.

## Where calendars struggle

### They do not know what the reminder is about

A calendar event is a title and a time. Unless you type the price, plan and where to cancel into every event, the reminder says little more than "Netflix".

### Changes mean editing every event

When a price goes up or you switch from monthly to yearly, you have to change the repeating event, and its description, by hand.

### No totals

A calendar cannot tell you that your subscriptions add up to a certain amount each month, or which category costs the most.

### Subscriptions get lost among meetings

Renewal reminders compete with work meetings, birthdays and appointments. They are easy to dismiss with everything else.

## What a subscription reminder app adds

RenewRadar is built for one job, so its reminders carry the details you need to decide:

- The service name and when it renews or when the trial ends.
- The price, and for trials, the price after the trial.
- Reminders at the timing you chose per subscription, such as 7 days and 1 day before.

![RenewRadar reminders with price details](fig:b)

Behind the reminders, the app keeps your monthly and yearly totals, spending by category and the next three renewal dates for each subscription. Changing a price is one edit, and every future reminder updates with it.

## Using both

If your calendar is where you plan your life, there is no harm in keeping a yearly renewal there too. Many people find that once their subscriptions are in a dedicated app, they no longer need calendar events for them.

## The difference in one sentence

A calendar reminds you that a date is coming. A subscription reminder app reminds you what that date will cost and gives you the context to decide.
