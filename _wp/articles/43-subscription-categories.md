Most people know their biggest single subscription. Fewer know their biggest subscription category. That second number is often more revealing.

## Why categories help

- **They reveal overlap.** Three cloud storage plans or two music services stand out immediately.
- **They show priorities.** If streaming costs more than fitness and learning combined, is that what you want?
- **They make goals concrete.** "Spend less on subscriptions" is vague. "Cut streaming from $45 to $25 a month" is a plan.

## The Stats screen

![Spending by category in RenewRadar](fig:a)

The Stats screen shows your monthly and yearly spending, split by category. A few details:

- **Only active, paid subscriptions count.** Free trials, paused and cancelled items are left out, so the numbers reflect what you actually pay.
- **Every cycle is normalised.** Weekly, quarterly and yearly plans are converted to monthly figures, so categories compare fairly.
- **Each currency gets its own totals.** RenewRadar does not guess exchange rates.

## How to use your category totals

### Look for duplicates

Check each category for services that do the same job. Two video services might both be worth it. Two cloud storage plans rarely are.

### Set a category budget

Pick a monthly amount for your biggest category and use it to choose between services, for example by rotating streaming services instead of keeping them all.

### Compare with value

A large software category may be fine if those tools earn you money. Mark them as Work or Business with the **Used for** label to keep them apart from personal spending.

## Choosing categories for unusual subscriptions

Not everything fits neatly. A rule of thumb: choose the category that matches why you pay, not what the company is. A VPN might be Utilities, a language course Other, and a podcast app Music. Consistency matters more than perfection.

## Start with the totals

Categories turn a list of charges into a picture of your priorities. Once you can see that picture, deciding what to keep becomes much easier.
