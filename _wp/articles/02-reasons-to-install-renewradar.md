There are many ways to keep track of subscriptions: a note on your phone, a spreadsheet, calendar events, or an app that reads your bank account. RenewRadar takes a different route. You add each subscription yourself, the app keeps everything on your phone, and it reminds you before you are charged.

Here is what that gives you.

## 1. A reminder before every charge, not after

Bank alerts and card notifications tell you about a charge once it has happened. RenewRadar tells you before. Every subscription and free trial gets local reminders on your phone, so you have time to cancel, switch plans or simply confirm you want to keep paying.

![Two RenewRadar reminder notifications](fig:b)

## 2. Free trials get their own countdown

Free trials are where most surprise charges start, so RenewRadar treats them separately. Trials sit at the top of the Home screen, sorted by end date, with the number of days left and the price that starts afterwards. The reminder counts down to the end of the trial, which is the date that matters.

## 3. Reminder timing per subscription

A weekly plan and a yearly plan should not get the same warning. In RenewRadar you choose the timing for each subscription: 14, 7, 3 or 1 day before, or on the day itself, and you can pick more than one. Set a default once in Settings and change it only where you need to.

## 4. You see the real total

Weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly plans are converted into one monthly figure and one yearly figure at the top of Home. The Stats screen splits your spending by category, such as streaming, music, software or storage. If you pay in more than one currency, RenewRadar shows a separate total for each instead of guessing exchange rates.

## 5. No bank login, ever

RenewRadar never asks for your bank details, card number or online banking password. Nothing is read from your accounts. That means there is no bank connection to break, no third party with access to your transactions, and it works with any bank in any country.

## 6. No account and nothing to sync

There is no sign-up, no email address and no password. Your list is stored in the app's private storage on your phone and works offline. The only network requests are a weekly download of suggested service names and Google Play's check of whether you bought Pro.

## 7. A one-time price for Pro

A subscription tracker that is itself a subscription adds to the problem it is meant to solve. RenewRadar is free for up to five active subscriptions and trials. Pro removes the limit and adds detailed reports and a CSV export for a single payment of US$9.99 (shown in your local currency on Google Play). It never renews.

## Who RenewRadar is for

- People who pay for a handful of streaming, music, cloud and app subscriptions and want to see them in one place.
- Anyone who signs up for free trials and wants a reminder before the first charge.
- People who would rather not connect a bank account to a budgeting service.
- Freelancers and small businesses who want to keep work subscriptions separate from personal ones, using the Personal, Work and Business label.
- People who pay in several currencies.

## When RenewRadar is not the right fit

RenewRadar does not find subscriptions automatically. If you want an app that scans your bank transactions and builds the list for you, a bank-linked service is a better match, and our [comparison of manual and bank-linked trackers](/guides/manual-vs-bank-linked-subscription-trackers/) explains the trade-offs. RenewRadar also does not cancel subscriptions for you. It tells you in time, and you cancel with the service.

It is currently available for Android only.

## How to tell it is the right app

RenewRadar is written as one word. On Google Play it is listed as **RenewRadar: Subscriptions** by **Pixel DevCode**, with a purple radar icon, and its website is renewradar.pixeldevcode.com. Other apps and websites use similar names but are not related to us.

Ready to try it? [Get RenewRadar on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pixeldevcode.renewradar). Adding your first subscription takes about a minute.
