If you add up your subscriptions and divide by twelve, you get a tidy monthly average. Real life is lumpier. A month with a yearly software renewal, a membership and an annual storage plan can cost several times more than a quiet month.

## Why some months are expensive

- **Yearly plans** all renew in the month you first bought them.
- **Holiday and new year sign-ups** cluster renewals in December and January.
- **Quarterly plans** add a bump every three months.
- **Intro offers ending** raise prices from a specific month.

## How to use it

![Steps to use the 12-month view](fig:b)

### Spot the peaks early

A yearly renewal you notice three months ahead is a decision. One you notice on the day is a surprise.

### Spread or move renewals

Some services let you switch billing dates or move from yearly to monthly. If three big renewals land in the same month, moving one can make budgeting easier.

### Set money aside

If you know April will cost an extra $140, putting a little aside each month is easier than finding it all at once.

### Decide before you are charged

When a big renewal is a month away, check whether you still use it, whether there is a cheaper plan, and whether a better deal exists. Then set a reminder 14 days before, so the final decision happens in time.
