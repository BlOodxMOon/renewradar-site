RenewRadar schedules reminders as local notifications on your phone. That means they do not depend on the internet or on a server, and they arrive even when the app is closed. On most phones this works perfectly.

Some Android phones, though, are very aggressive about saving battery. They may pause apps you have not opened recently and hold their notifications until you open them again. For a subscription reminder app, a late reminder can mean a charge you wanted to avoid.

## Signs your phone is delaying reminders

- Reminders appear only when you open RenewRadar.
- Several reminders arrive at once.
- A reminder arrives hours after the time you expected.

Phones from Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, OPPO, realme, vivo, OnePlus and Huawei are the most likely to need a change, but any phone with battery optimization can be affected.

## The fix: allow RenewRadar to run without battery restrictions

![General steps to remove battery restrictions](fig:a)

This setting does not make RenewRadar use more battery. The app does almost nothing in the background. It only allows the phone to deliver the notifications that are already scheduled.

## Where the setting is on popular phones

![Where to find the battery setting on popular phones](fig:b)

On Xiaomi phones running HyperOS or MIUI, also check **Autostart** in the app's settings and turn it on if it is available.

## Check notification permissions too

1. Open **RenewRadar**, then **Settings**. It shows whether notifications are allowed.
2. If they are not, tap through to the system screen and allow them.
3. In your phone's notification settings, make sure the RenewRadar reminder channel is not silenced.

## Test it

The simplest test is to add a test subscription with a renewal date tomorrow and a reminder **on the day**. Lock your phone, leave the app closed, and see whether the reminder arrives. Delete the test item afterwards.

## Do not use "Clear all" on recent apps for RenewRadar

On some phones, swiping an app away from the recent apps list or using a cleaner app can stop its scheduled notifications. If you use a cleaner, add RenewRadar to its list of protected apps, or lock it in the recent apps screen.

## Why this matters for a subscription tracker app

Most apps can afford a delayed notification. A subscription reminder cannot, because it is tied to a date when money leaves your account. Two minutes of setup ensures every reminder for every subscription and free trial arrives when you planned it.
