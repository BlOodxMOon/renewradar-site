January is when gym memberships, fitness apps, language courses and productivity tools see the most sign-ups. It is also when holiday trials end and yearly plans from last January renew. That makes the first week of the year the perfect time for a subscription reset.

![A new year subscription reset](fig:a)

## 1. Cancel one thing you did not use

Start small. Look at your list and find one subscription you did not use in December. Cancel it today. One cancellation is a real saving and builds momentum.

## 2. Check every holiday trial

Trials started in December end in January. List them all, with end dates and prices after the trial. Decide now which you will keep. See our guide on [holiday trials ending in January](/guides/holiday-free-trials-january/).

## 3. Set reminders for every yearly plan

Yearly plans are the most expensive renewals and the easiest to forget. Make sure each one has a reminder at least two weeks before it renews.

## 4. Give resolution apps a check-in date

Signing up for a fitness or language app in January is great. Make it a deliberate experiment: if it starts with a free trial, track the end date. If it is paid monthly, set a reminder before the second charge and ask, honestly, whether you used it.

![Reminders for resolution apps](fig:b)

## 5. Write down your yearly total

Note what your subscriptions cost per year at the start of January. Check again in June and December. Watching the number over time is one of the simplest ways to keep it reasonable.
