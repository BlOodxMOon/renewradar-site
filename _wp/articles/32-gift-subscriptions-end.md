Gift subscriptions are an easy present: a gift card for a streaming service, a year of a music app, a few months of a box delivery. But they work in different ways, and the end of the gift is not always the end of the payments.

## Two kinds of gift subscriptions

![Two kinds of gift subscriptions](fig:a)

### Prepaid gifts

The giver pays once for a fixed period, often with a gift card or code. When the receiver redeems it, some services ask for a payment method. If the receiver adds one, the subscription can continue automatically at the regular price when the gift period ends.

### Gifted plans

The giver adds someone to their own plan or pays for a subscription in the receiver's name. In this case, the giver keeps paying until they cancel, sometimes long after the receiver stopped using it.

## If you received a gift subscription

![Steps for receivers](fig:b)

The gift period is essentially a free trial with a longer end date. Treat it like one:

1. **Check your account** to see whether a payment method is on file.
2. **Find the exact end date** of the gift period.
3. **Track it.** In RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, add it with **Free trial** switched on, the gift end date as the trial end, and the regular price as the price after the trial. It appears in the Trials section with a countdown.
4. **Set reminders** a week and a day before the gift ends.

When the reminder arrives, decide whether you want to keep paying yourself.

## If you gave a gift subscription

If you bought a one-time prepaid gift, there is usually nothing to track. If you pay for someone on your own plan, or bought a subscription that renews, add it to your tracker:

- Set **Used for** to Personal and write who it is for in the notes, such as "Gift for Mum".
- Choose a reminder before each renewal, so you can check whether it is still used and wanted.

## Gift cards for app stores

A Google Play gift card adds credit to the receiver's account. Subscriptions paid from that balance continue as long as there is credit, and some then switch to the next payment method on file. If you use a Play balance for subscriptions, keep an eye on the renewal dates as the balance runs down.

## Give the gift of fewer surprises

When giving a subscription, a short note helps: "This runs until March. Cancel before then if you do not want to keep it." It is a small kindness that saves the receiver a surprise charge.
