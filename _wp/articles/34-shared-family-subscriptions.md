Family and duo plans let several people share one subscription for much less than separate plans. Music, video, cloud storage, games and even some software offer them. They are a great deal, as long as everyone knows how they work.

## The common mix-ups

![Common shared-plan mix-ups](fig:a)

- **Paying twice.** Someone joins the family plan but forgets to cancel their own individual plan.
- **Paying for someone who left.** A family member moved out or stopped using it, but is still on the plan.
- **Unclear billing.** Nobody is sure whose card the plan is on.
- **Trials on the wrong card.** A child or partner starts a trial using a shared payment method.

## Decide who tracks what

The simplest rule: **the person who pays tracks the subscription.** Everyone else does not need to, unless they reimburse the payer.

If you pay for a family plan, add it to your subscription tracker with the full price. If someone pays you back each month, note it.

## Tracking shared plans in RenewRadar

RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, does not need accounts or sharing to handle family plans. Each person keeps their own list on their own phone, and the payer tracks the shared plan.

![Steps to track a shared plan](fig:b)

A few tips:

- **Use the notes field** for who is on the plan and who pays what, such as "Family plan: me, Ana, Leo. Ana pays $5."
- **Set a reminder before the renewal** to check that everyone still uses it.
- **When someone joins your plan,** remind them to cancel their own individual subscription, and to mark it as cancelled in their RenewRadar.

## Google Play family payment methods

Google Play lets families share a payment method through a family group. Purchases and subscriptions by family members can be charged to that shared method, sometimes with approval settings for children. If you manage a family group, review its subscriptions in Google Play from time to time and track the ones you pay for.

## Splitting costs fairly

If you split a plan, the yearly total helps conversations stay simple: "This costs $203.88 a year, so $67.96 each." RenewRadar shows each subscription's monthly and yearly cost, which makes splitting easy to calculate.

## Review once a year

Family situations change. Once a year, check each shared plan: who is on it, who uses it, and whether a cheaper plan now fits better.
