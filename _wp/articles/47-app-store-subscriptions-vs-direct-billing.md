Most popular services let you subscribe in more than one way: inside the Android app through Google Play, inside the iPhone app through Apple, or on their website with a card or PayPal. The service is the same, but the billing is not.

![Three ways to be billed](fig:a)

## Why it matters

### Cancelling

You cancel where you are billed. A subscription bought through Google Play cannot be cancelled on the service's website, and a website subscription will not appear in Google Play. Uninstalling the app cancels nothing.

### Refunds

Refund requests go to whoever billed you: Google, Apple or the service. Each has different rules.

### Price

Prices can differ. App store prices are set in local currency and may include taxes. Website prices are sometimes lower because the service avoids app store fees, but may be in US dollars, with card fees for foreign currency.

### Visibility

App store subscriptions are listed in one place: Google Play's or Apple's Subscriptions page. Website subscriptions are spread across many accounts and emails.

## How to find out where you are billed

- **Your statement:** "Google Play" or "Apple.com/bill" in the description means app store billing.
- **The confirmation email:** the sender shows who billed you.
- **Google Play's Subscriptions page:** if it is listed there, Play bills you.

## Keep a note of it

The simplest habit: write down where each subscription is billed when you add it to your tracker.

In RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, use the notes field, for example "Google Play", "Website, Visa ending 4421" or "PayPal". When a reminder arrives and you decide to cancel, you know exactly where to go.

## Which should you choose?

- **App store billing** is convenient: one place to see and cancel everything, and familiar refund processes.
- **Website billing** can be cheaper and gives access to plans that app stores do not offer.

Either is fine, as long as you know which you chose. Our guide to [cancelling a free trial](/guides/cancel-free-trial-before-charged/) covers the steps for each billing method.
