Cancelling a subscription feels like the end of the story. Often it is not. Charges can continue by mistake, you may want to return later, and it helps to remember what you stopped paying for.

![Reasons to keep cancelled subscriptions](fig:a)

## Catch charges that should have stopped

Sometimes a cancellation does not go through: a confirmation was never completed, a family member re-subscribed, or the service made a mistake. If you have a record of what you cancelled and when, a charge that should not be there stands out immediately, and you have the date to quote when you ask for a refund.

## Remember what you paid

Prices rise. If you return to a service a year later, knowing what you used to pay helps you judge the new price, or ask for a returning-customer offer.

## Rejoin easily when you rotate

If you rotate streaming services, you will cancel and restart the same ones throughout the year. Keeping them in your tracker means you do not have to re-enter them every time.

## See your progress

A list of cancelled subscriptions is a list of money you are no longer spending. It is satisfying, and it is a good reminder of why you keep track.

## How RenewRadar handles cancelled subscriptions

In RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, open a subscription and tap **Mark as cancelled** after you cancel with the service.

![Cancelled items in RenewRadar](fig:b)

- The item leaves your active list and appears under **Cancelled** at the bottom of Home.
- It stops counting in your monthly and yearly totals.
- It does not count toward the free limit of five, so you can keep as many as you like for free.
- Reminders for it are removed.

To bring one back, open it from the Cancelled list and tap **Restore**. RenewRadar sets it back to active and moves a past renewal date forward, so you only need to check the price and date.

## A small habit

When you cancel, add a note with the date and how you cancelled, such as "Cancelled Jan 3 in Google Play". If something goes wrong later, you have everything you need in one place.
