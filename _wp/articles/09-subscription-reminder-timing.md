Many reminder tools give you a single alert, usually one day before. That works for some subscriptions and fails for others. A yearly software plan that costs hundreds deserves more notice than a weekly app.

RenewRadar, the [subscription reminder app](/subscription-reminder-app/) for Android, lets you set the timing for each subscription separately.

## Why timing matters

A reminder is only useful if it arrives at a moment when you can act. That depends on three things:

- **How much is at stake.** Bigger charges deserve more time to think and compare.
- **How long cancelling takes.** Some services need several steps, or a notice period before the renewal date.
- **How often you are charged.** For a weekly plan, a reminder two weeks ahead would cover two other charges.

## Suggested timing

![Suggested reminder timing by subscription type](fig:a)

These are starting points, not rules. Use earlier reminders for anything you are unsure about.

### Yearly plans

Yearly renewals are the most expensive and the easiest to forget, because they happen once a year. Two reminders work well: one about two weeks before, which gives time to compare prices or switch to monthly, and one a few days before as a final check.

### Monthly plans

Three days and one day before is a good default. It is late enough to be relevant and early enough to cancel online.

### Weekly plans

A single reminder a day before is usually enough. Weekly subscriptions are short, and you will see the next charge soon.

### Free trials

Trials need reminders before the trial ends, not before a renewal. Three days and one day before give you a warning and a last call. If you already know you do not want to keep the service, cancel right after signing up.

## Why two reminders beat one

A single notification is easy to swipe away while you are busy. A second one, a few days later, gives you another chance. It also turns the first reminder into a gentle question and the second into a decision.

## How it works in RenewRadar

When you add or edit a subscription, choose any combination of **14 days**, **7 days**, **3 days**, **1 day** before, or **on the day**. The form shows a plain summary, such as "You will be reminded 14 days and 3 days before."

You can set default reminders in Settings, which new subscriptions use automatically, and change them for single items whenever you like.

Reminders are scheduled as notifications on your phone. They arrive without the app open and without an internet connection. After each renewal, RenewRadar rolls the date forward and schedules the next reminders for you.
