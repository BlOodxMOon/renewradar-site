Streaming used to be one service. Now the shows you want are spread across several, each with its own monthly price. Paying for all of them all year adds up quickly. Subscription rotation is a simple alternative: subscribe to one or two at a time, watch what you want, then switch.

## How rotation works

1. **Pick the service with the most you want to watch right now.**
2. **Subscribe for a month.** Binge the series or films you came for.
3. **Cancel before it renews.** Most services let you keep watching until the end of the paid month.
4. **Move to the next service** when something new arrives there.

![A simple rotation plan](fig:a)

## Why it saves money

If you pay for four services every month, you pay for four. If you rotate two at a time, you pay for two, and you still watch everything, just not all at once. Over a year, the difference can be several hundred dollars, depending on your services and country.

## What makes rotation fail

Rotation only works if you actually cancel. The most common way it fails:

- You forget to cancel the old service when you start a new one.
- You cancel, but sign up again for one show and forget that one too.
- You lose track of which services are active right now.

## Tracking a rotation

This is where a subscription tracker app helps. In RenewRadar:

- Keep every streaming service you use in the app, active or not.
- When you switch away from one, cancel it with the service, then **mark it as cancelled**. It moves to the Cancelled list and stops counting in your totals.
- When you come back, open the Cancelled list and **restore** it. RenewRadar sets it back to active and moves a past renewal date forward.
- Set a reminder a few days before each renewal, so you can decide whether to stay another month.

## Tips for smooth rotation

- **Plan around release schedules.** Subscribe when a full season is available rather than week by week.
- **Watch for annual-only offers** that do not fit rotation.
- **Check what your bundles include** before subscribing separately.
- **Use free trials once,** and track them like any other trial.

## Is rotation worth the effort?

For people who watch a lot of different content, yes. It takes a few minutes a month and a reliable reminder. For people who mostly use one service, keeping that one and dropping the rest is even simpler.

Either way, knowing exactly which services are active, and getting a reminder before each one renews, is what keeps the savings real.
