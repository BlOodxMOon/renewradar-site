Black Friday is not just about televisions and headphones. Streaming services, music apps, software and cloud storage all use the week to offer extended free trials, three months for a single dollar, or a big discount on the first year.

These offers can be genuinely good. The catch is that most of them become regular subscriptions at full price when the offer ends, and you may start several at once.

## Why Black Friday trials are easy to forget

- **You start several in one week.** Each has a different length and end date.
- **The end dates land in a busy season.** Many three-month offers end around late February, when nobody is thinking about Black Friday.
- **The price after the offer is in the small print.** Ads show the deal price, not the renewal price.
- **Some offers switch to yearly billing.** One forgotten offer can mean a full year's charge.

## Four questions before you accept any offer

![Questions to ask before accepting an offer](fig:a)

If you cannot find the answer to these questions on the offer page, check the terms or the confirmation email before you continue.

## A simple plan for deal week

1. **Make a short list of services you actually want to try.** A deal on something you will not use is not a deal.
2. **Prefer offers you can cancel immediately.** On Google Play, you can usually cancel a trial right after starting it and still keep access until the end.
3. **Record every offer the day you start it.** Name, end date, price afterwards, and where you signed up.
4. **Set two reminders for each:** one a week before the end, one a day or two before.
5. **Decide early.** Give each service a fair try in the first weeks, then decide before the last few days.

RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app, is well suited to this. Add each offer as a free trial with its end date and the price after the trial. If the deal is a cheap first period rather than a free one, add it as a subscription with the renewal date when the full price starts, and note the regular price in the notes field.

## After the offer ends

When a reminder arrives, choose one of three things:

- **Keep it** at the new price. RenewRadar keeps tracking the renewal.
- **Cancel it** where you signed up, then mark it as cancelled in RenewRadar. Cancelled items stay in a separate list and do not count toward the free limit.
- **Switch to a cheaper plan**, and update the price in the app.
