If you search for a subscription tracker app, you will find two very different kinds of product. Some connect to your bank and find recurring payments automatically. Others, like RenewRadar, let you add subscriptions yourself and keep everything on your phone.

Both approaches solve the same problem, forgotten charges, but they make different trade-offs.

## How bank-linked trackers work

A bank-linked tracker asks you to connect one or more bank or card accounts through a data aggregator. It then scans your transactions, spots payments that repeat, and builds your subscription list. Many of these apps are full budgeting tools, with spending categories, balances and sometimes services that negotiate bills or cancel subscriptions for you.

**Strengths:** little typing, finds subscriptions you forgot about, shows your wider spending.

**Weaknesses:** the app sees all your transactions, it usually requires an account, it works only with supported banks and countries, and it learns about a subscription after the first charge appears.

## How manual trackers work

A manual tracker, also called a subscription reminder app, lets you add each subscription with its price, billing cycle and next date. It then reminds you before each renewal and shows totals.

**Strengths:** no bank connection, no account, works in any country and currency, and it can track free trials before any money is charged.

**Weaknesses:** you add subscriptions yourself, and you need to update an entry if a price changes.

## The free trial gap

This difference matters more than it first seems. A bank-linked app sees a subscription only when a charge shows up on your statement. For a free trial, that is too late: the first charge is the one you wanted to avoid.

A manual tracker knows about the trial from the day you start it, because you add it then. RenewRadar puts trials in their own section with a countdown and reminders before the trial ends.

## Privacy and security

With a bank-linked app, your transaction history is shared with the app and usually with the aggregator that connects it. Reputable services protect that data carefully, but it still exists outside your bank.

With a manual tracker like RenewRadar, your list never leaves your phone. There is no server holding it and no account that could be breached.

## Cost

Many bank-linked services are free to start and charge a monthly subscription for premium features. RenewRadar is free for up to five subscriptions, and Pro is a one-time payment of US$9.99, shown in your local currency on Google Play.

## Which one should you choose?

![Four questions to help you choose](fig:b)

Choose a **bank-linked tracker** if you want subscriptions found automatically, your bank is supported, you are comfortable sharing transactions and you also want a full budgeting tool.

Choose a **manual tracker like RenewRadar** if you would rather not share bank data, you live outside the countries bank-linked apps support, you often start free trials, or you pay in more than one currency.
