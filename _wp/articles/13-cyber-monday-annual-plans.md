Cyber Monday is the day many software, streaming and app companies offer their biggest discounts on yearly plans. Thirty, forty or even fifty percent off the first year is common.

A good annual deal can save you money. But a yearly plan is also a commitment, and the second year is usually charged at full price.

## The break-even question

Before buying any yearly plan, compare it with the monthly price. Divide the yearly price by the monthly price. The answer is the number of months you need to keep using the service for the yearly plan to pay off.

![Break-even point for a yearly plan](fig:a)

With a deep Cyber Monday discount, the break-even point can drop to five or six months. That is a good deal for a service you already use every week. For something you have never tried, it is a gamble.

## Signs an annual deal is worth it

- You have used the service regularly for several months.
- The discounted yearly price breaks even well within a year.
- You would be paying monthly anyway.
- The service lets you cancel the renewal right away and keep access until the end of the year.

## Signs to skip it

- You are buying it because of the discount, not the service.
- You have not used the monthly plan for a while.
- The deal is for a service you already pay for through a bundle or a family plan.
- The terms say the price after the first year is much higher.

## The real risk is next year

The biggest cost of a Cyber Monday plan is often not the first payment. It is the second one, a year later, at full price, when you have forgotten why you bought it.

Yearly renewals are easy to miss because they happen once. There is no monthly charge to remind you the subscription exists.

![The timeline of a yearly renewal](fig:b)

## How to protect yourself

1. **Note the full price after the first year** as soon as you buy.
2. **Turn off auto-renew immediately** if the service allows it without losing access. You can always renew later on purpose.
3. **Add the plan to a subscription tracker** with the renewal date a year from now.
4. **Set an early reminder.** Two weeks before is a good choice for yearly plans, with a second reminder a few days before.

In RenewRadar, add the plan with a yearly billing cycle, the renewal date and the renewal price. Choose reminders 14 and 3 days before. The monthly total on Home shows the plan as a twelfth of the yearly price, so it stays visible in your budget all year.

## The bottom line

Annual deals reward commitment to services you already value. Buy them deliberately, track the renewal from day one, and decide again next year with a reminder in hand.
