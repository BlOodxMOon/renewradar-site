RenewRadar is designed to be set up once and then stay out of your way. You do not need an account, and you do not need to connect a bank. This guide walks you through the first five minutes.

![Five setup steps](fig:a)

## 1. Install and allow notifications

Install **RenewRadar: Subscriptions** by Pixel DevCode from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pixeldevcode.renewradar). When the app asks to send notifications, tap **Allow**. Reminders are local notifications on your phone, so this permission is what makes them work.

If you skipped it, open **Settings** in RenewRadar. It shows whether notifications are allowed and takes you to the right system screen.

## 2. Choose your defaults

In **Settings**, set two things:

- **Default currency:** the currency you pay in most often. You can still choose a different currency for any single subscription.
- **Default reminders:** when you want to be reminded before a renewal. Choose any combination of 14, 7, 3 or 1 day before, or on the day. Three days and one day before is a good start.

New subscriptions use these defaults, so you rarely need to change them per item.

## 3. Add your first subscription

Tap the add button on Home and start typing the name. Suggestions for popular services appear as you type and fill in the category and usual billing cycle. Then enter:

- **Price**, and the currency if it differs from your default.
- **Billing cycle:** weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly, or a custom number of days.
- **Next renewal date:** the date of the next charge.
- **Used for:** Personal, Work or Business, if you want to keep them apart.
- **Notes**, such as which card or account it is billed to.

Save, and the subscription appears on Home, sorted by the next charge.

## 4. Add free trials the right way

For a trial, switch on **Free trial**, then enter the trial end date and the price after the trial. Trials appear in their own section at the top of Home with a countdown, and reminders count down to the end of the trial.

![Adding a free trial in RenewRadar](fig:b)

## 5. Check your totals

Once your subscriptions are in, the top of Home shows your monthly and yearly totals. Open **Stats** to see spending by category. This is often the moment people find something to cancel.

## Make sure reminders reach you

Some Android phones, especially those with aggressive battery saving, can delay notifications from apps that are not opened often. If a reminder ever arrives late, set RenewRadar's battery usage to **Unrestricted** or **No restrictions** in your phone's app settings. Our guide on [making reminders reliable on Android](/guides/subscription-reminders-android-battery/) has the steps for common phone brands.

## What happens next

You do not need to open RenewRadar every day. After a renewal date passes, the next one is calculated automatically, and reminders are rescheduled. Open the app when a reminder arrives, when you start a new trial, or when a price changes.

The free version covers up to five active subscriptions and trials. If you track more, Pro removes the limit with a one-time payment.
