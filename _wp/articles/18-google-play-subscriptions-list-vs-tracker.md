If you use Android, Google Play has a handy page: open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then **Payments & subscriptions** and **Subscriptions**. It lists active subscriptions billed through Google Play, with renewal dates and a Cancel button.

So why would you need a separate subscription tracker app?

## What Google Play shows

The Play list includes subscriptions and free trials that you bought inside Android apps and paid for with your Google Play payment method. It is the right place to cancel those subscriptions, and our [guide to cancelling on Google Play](/guides/cancel-google-play-subscription/) walks through it.

## What Google Play does not show

Many of the services people pay for never go through Google Play.

![Subscriptions that Google Play does not list](fig:a)

Streaming services, software and news sites often push you to sign up on their websites, where they avoid app store fees. Those subscriptions are billed directly to your card or PayPal and do not appear in Google Play at all.

## What else is missing

Even for subscriptions it does list, the Play page is not designed to help you plan:

- **No custom reminders.** Google Play may email you about some renewals, but you cannot choose when or how often.
- **No totals.** It does not add up what you pay each month or year.
- **No history** of the subscriptions you cancelled.
- **No trial countdowns** with the price after the trial on one screen.

![Google Play's list and RenewRadar compared](fig:b)

## How RenewRadar complements Google Play

RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, is where you keep everything in one place, wherever it is billed. A handy note to add for each subscription is where it is billed, such as "Google Play", "website, Visa" or "PayPal", so you know exactly where to cancel.

When a reminder arrives for a subscription billed through Play, you cancel in the Play Store. For everything else, you cancel on the service's website or with whoever bills you. RenewRadar does not cancel subscriptions itself, but it makes sure you know in time.

## A five-minute check

1. Open the Google Play **Subscriptions** page and add each item to RenewRadar.
2. Search your email for "receipt" and "renewal" to find subscriptions billed elsewhere.
3. Add those too, with a note saying where each one is billed.

You now have a complete list, with reminders for all of it, not just the part Google Play knows about.
