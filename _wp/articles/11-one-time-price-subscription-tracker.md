The point of a subscription tracker is to stop money leaking out in small, recurring amounts. So it is strange when the tracker itself becomes one of those recurring amounts. A few dollars a month does not sound like much, but that is exactly how every other subscription starts.

## The cost over time

A subscription that costs a little each month adds up quietly. Over three years, even a modest monthly price can cost many times more than a one-time purchase.

![Three-year cost of a one-time price and monthly prices](fig:a)

That is the same arithmetic RenewRadar shows you for every other service you pay for. It would be odd to hide it for ourselves.

## Why RenewRadar can be a one-time purchase

Recurring prices make sense when an app has recurring costs: servers, bank data connections or cloud storage for every user. RenewRadar has almost none of those.

- **Your list is stored on your phone**, not on our servers.
- **There is no bank connection**, so there are no data aggregator fees.
- **There is no account system** to run and secure.
- **Reminders are local notifications**, scheduled by your phone.

Because the app does not depend on paid services per user, a single payment can cover it fairly. If we ever add a feature with real ongoing costs, such as optional cloud sync, we would explain clearly what it costs and why. Pro as it is today stays a one-time purchase.

## What is free and what is Pro

RenewRadar is useful without paying anything. The free version includes everything you need to avoid surprise charges for up to five active subscriptions and free trials. Cancelled items do not count toward the limit.

![Free and Pro compared](fig:b)

Pro is a one-time purchase of US$9.99, shown in your local currency on Google Play. It removes the limit and adds detailed reports, including the charges you can expect over the next 12 months, and a CSV export of your full list.

## How the purchase works

Pro is bought through Google Play, like any other in-app purchase. It is linked to your Google account, not to an account with us. If you get a new phone, install RenewRadar, open Settings and tap **Restore purchase** to unlock Pro again.

Your subscription list itself is stored on the phone, so before switching phones, Pro users can export a CSV copy.

## Is Pro worth it?

If you track five or fewer things, the free version may be all you ever need. If you track more, think of Pro the way RenewRadar would: one payment, compared with the cost of a single forgotten renewal. Many yearly plans cost more than Pro on their own.

Either way, you will never get a reminder from RenewRadar about RenewRadar renewing, because it does not.
