December is when streaming services release holiday films, finish big seasons and run their most generous trials. It is also when many people sign up for two or three services they forget about until the January statement.

A short plan avoids that.

## Step 1: Make a watch list

Before signing up for anything, write down what you actually want to watch over the holidays: the Christmas classics, the new season, the big match. Next to each, note which service has it. Many titles are on services you already pay for.

## Step 2: Choose one or two services for December

Group your list by service. Usually one or two services cover most of it. Subscribe to those for December and leave the rest for another month.

![A holiday streaming plan](fig:a)

## Step 3: Use trials on purpose

Holiday trials are a good way to watch a specific series or film for free. Use them for what is on your list, not for browsing.

- **Cancel right after signing up** if the service allows it. On Google Play, you usually keep access until the end of the trial.
- **Note the end date and the price after the trial** straight away.
- **Avoid trials that convert to yearly plans** unless you are sure.

## Step 4: Track every holiday subscription

Holiday sign-ups are the ones most often forgotten, because they happen during a busy, distracted time and end in January.

Add each trial and new subscription to RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, the day you start it. Trials appear at the top of Home with a countdown and the price after the trial. Set reminders three days and one day before each one ends.

![Reminders for holiday subscriptions in January](fig:b)

## Step 5: Decide in early January

When the reminders arrive, choose for each service:

- **Keep it** if you will keep watching.
- **Cancel it** if the holiday binge is over, then mark it as cancelled in RenewRadar.
- **Rotate**, keeping one service for January and switching later. See our guide to [subscription rotation](/guides/subscription-rotation/).

## Do not forget sports passes and add-ons

Holiday sports passes, premium channels and add-ons often renew monthly at a higher price. Track them like any other subscription, with an early reminder.

## Enjoy the holidays

The goal is not to watch less. It is to make sure December's entertainment does not quietly become February's bill.
