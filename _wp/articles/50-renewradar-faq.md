## What is RenewRadar?

RenewRadar is a subscription tracker and reminder app for Android, made by Pixel DevCode. You add your subscriptions and free trials, and it shows what they cost per month and per year and reminds you before each charge.

![RenewRadar in short](fig:b)

## Is RenewRadar related to Renew Radar or Renewal Radar?

No. Several products use similar names. RenewRadar, written as one word, is the subscription tracker app by Pixel DevCode, and renewradar.pixeldevcode.com is its only website. It is not affiliated with renewradar.com, which is an SSL certificate monitoring service, or with other apps that have similar names.

![How to recognise the real RenewRadar](fig:a)

## Is RenewRadar free?

Yes, for up to five subscriptions and free trials, with reminders, trial countdowns and totals. Cancelled items do not count toward the limit. Pro removes the limit and adds detailed reports and CSV export.

## How much is Pro?

A one-time payment of US$9.99, shown in your local currency on Google Play. It is not a subscription and never renews.

## Does it connect to my bank?

No. RenewRadar never asks for bank logins or card details. You add each subscription yourself, which takes about a minute with suggestions for popular services.

## Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, email or password. Your list is stored only on your phone.

## When do reminders arrive?

You choose for each subscription: 14, 7, 3 or 1 day before, or on the day, at 9 in the morning. You can set defaults in Settings. Reminders are local notifications, so they work without the app open and without internet.

## How are free trials handled?

Switch on **Free trial** when you add a service, then enter the trial end date and the price after the trial. Trials appear at the top of Home with a countdown and reminders before they end.

## Which currencies are supported?

Any currency with a three-letter code. If you use more than one, RenewRadar shows separate totals per currency instead of guessing exchange rates.

## Can RenewRadar cancel subscriptions for me?

No. It reminds you in time, and you cancel with the service: in Google Play, your Apple account or on the service's website.

## Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. RenewRadar is available for Android on Google Play.

## What happens if I change phones?

Your list stays on the old phone, because nothing is synced to a server. Pro users can export a CSV copy. Pro itself is restored on a new phone with **Restore purchase** in Settings, using the same Google account.

## My reminders arrive late. What can I do?

Some Android phones delay notifications to save battery. Set RenewRadar's battery usage to unrestricted. Our guide to [reliable reminders on Android](/guides/subscription-reminders-android-battery/) has the steps.
