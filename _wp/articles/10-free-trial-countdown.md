Most subscription lists treat everything the same way: a name, a price and a next date. That works for subscriptions you already pay for. It does not work well for free trials, because the most important number for a trial is not the price. It is how many days you have left to decide.

## A trial is a deadline, not a bill

When a regular subscription renews, you are paying for something you already chose. When a trial ends, you are making a new decision, often without realising it. If you do nothing, you agree to pay.

That changes what you need to see:

- **The end date**, not the renewal date.
- **The days left**, so you know how urgent it is.
- **The price after the trial**, which may be monthly or yearly.

## Why a countdown works better than a date

"Ends on November 30" requires you to know today's date and do the arithmetic. "2 days left" tells you immediately whether you need to act now.

![A countdown is easier to act on](fig:b)

Countdowns also make trials sort naturally. The one ending soonest is the one to look at first.

## How RenewRadar shows free trials

In RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, a subscription marked as a free trial goes to a separate **Trials** section at the top of Home. Each trial shows:

- The number of days left, such as "2 days left".
- The end date.
- The price that starts after the trial, such as "then $11.99/mo".

Trials are sorted by end date. The totals at the top of Home count only what you actually pay, so a trial does not inflate your monthly spend until it becomes a paid subscription.

![The Trials section in RenewRadar](fig:a)

## Reminders before the trial ends

Trial reminders count down to the end of the trial. If you choose three days and one day before, you will get a notification three days before the trial ends and another the day before. Each notification includes the price after the trial, so the decision is clear.

## What happens when a trial ends

When the end date passes, the trial stays in the Trials section marked as **Ended**, so it does not silently disappear. Then you choose:

- **Keep it.** Edit the item, switch off Free trial and set the next renewal date. From then on it is tracked as a regular subscription.
- **Cancel it.** Cancel with the service, then mark it as cancelled in RenewRadar. It moves to the Cancelled list.

## Tips for trial-heavy months

- Add trials on the day you start them, before you forget the exact end date.
- If you sign up through Google Play, you can usually cancel immediately and still use the trial until it ends.
- Watch out for trials that switch to annual billing. Our guide to [how free trials turn into charges](/guides/how-free-trials-turn-into-charges/) explains the common traps.

A countdown turns a free trial back into what it should be: a choice you make on purpose.
