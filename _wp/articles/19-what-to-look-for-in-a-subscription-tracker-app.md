Subscription tracker apps range from simple lists to full budgeting tools. Some have nearly identical names. Before you trust one with reminders for money leaving your account, it is worth checking a few things.

## 1. Does it remind you before charges?

This is the core job. Look for reminders before each renewal, not just a list. Check that reminders work without opening the app.

## 2. Can you set reminder timing per subscription?

A yearly plan needs an earlier warning than a weekly one. The best apps let you choose the timing for each subscription and set a default.

## 3. Does it treat free trials differently?

Trials have an end date, not a renewal date, and the price after the trial matters. Look for a separate trial section with a countdown.

## 4. Does it show real totals?

The app should convert weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly plans into comparable numbers and show monthly and yearly totals, ideally by category.

## 5. Does it handle your currencies?

If you pay in more than one currency, check whether the app supports that, and how it calculates totals. Guessed exchange rates can make totals look precise but wrong.

## 6. Does it need your bank login?

Some apps need a bank connection to work. That can be convenient, but it means sharing all your transactions. Decide whether you are comfortable with that, and whether your bank is supported.

## 7. Where is your data stored?

Read the privacy policy. Does the app need an account? Is your list stored on a server, or only on your phone? Does it contain ads or trackers?

## 8. Is the price fair and clear?

A subscription tracker that charges a monthly subscription adds to the problem. Check what is free, what is paid, and whether the paid version is a one-time purchase or a recurring one.

## 9. Who makes it, and is it maintained?

Look at the developer name, the website and the update history on Google Play. An app with a real website, a privacy policy, a contact address and recent updates is more likely to keep working. Similar app names are common, so check the developer before you install.
