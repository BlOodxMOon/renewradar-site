Weekly subscriptions are common in mobile apps: photo editors, VPNs, language learning, fitness and AI tools. The price is shown per week to make it look small. But there are 52 weeks in a year, and about 4.33 in a month.

![A weekly price over a year](fig:a)

## Doing the arithmetic

To compare a weekly plan with monthly or yearly options:

- **Monthly cost:** weekly price x 52 / 12.
- **Yearly cost:** weekly price x 52.

A $2.99 weekly plan is about $12.96 a month and $155.48 a year. If the same app offers a yearly plan for $39.99, the weekly plan costs almost four times as much over a year.

![The same app paid weekly, monthly and yearly](fig:b)

## Why apps use weekly plans

- **A small number on the purchase screen** feels easy to accept.
- **Short trials lead into them.** A three-day trial followed by a weekly plan is a common pattern.
- **People forget them.** Weekly charges are frequent but small, so each one barely registers.

## When a weekly plan makes sense

- You need the app for a short project, a trip or a single week.
- You will cancel straight after.

For anything you will use longer than a month or two, a monthly or yearly plan is almost always cheaper.

## How to spot weekly plans you already have

Check Google Play's Subscriptions page. The price shows the billing period, such as "$2.99/week". Also look at your statements for small charges that repeat every seven days.

## Tracking weekly plans in RenewRadar

In RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, choose **Weekly** as the billing cycle. The app converts it to a monthly figure in your totals using the same formula, weekly x 52 / 12, so a weekly plan appears at its true monthly cost next to everything else.

A reminder one day before each weekly charge is usually enough. If you only need the app for a short time, set a reminder and cancel as soon as you are done.

## The takeaway

Always convert a weekly price into a monthly or yearly cost before you accept it. If you will use the app for more than a few weeks, choose the longer plan, or look for an alternative without a weekly price.
