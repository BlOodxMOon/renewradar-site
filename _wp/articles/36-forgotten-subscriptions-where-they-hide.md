Most people can list their main subscriptions from memory. The ones that cost money for months without anyone noticing are those billed in unusual places. Here are nine places to look.

![Nine places to check](fig:a)

## 1. Google Play

Play Store, profile picture, **Payments & subscriptions**, **Subscriptions**. Check every Google account you use, not just the main one.

## 2. Apple

If you have ever had an iPhone or iPad, check **Settings**, your name, **Subscriptions**, or your Apple account on the web. Subscriptions keep running even after you switch to Android.

## 3. PayPal and other wallets

PayPal keeps a list of **automatic payments** in its settings. Many services you signed up for with "Pay with PayPal" are there.

## 4. Card and bank statements

Go through the last three months and look for amounts that repeat. Yearly charges will not show up in three months, so also search the last year for well-known service names.

## 5. Your phone carrier bill

Carriers can bill games, streaming bundles and premium services directly to your phone bill. These are easy to miss because they are combined with your plan.

## 6. Old email accounts

Search old inboxes for "receipt", "renewal", "subscription", "trial" and "your plan". Services keep billing even if you no longer read that email address.

## 7. Memberships with perks

Shopping memberships, delivery passes and warehouse clubs often renew yearly and include streaming or music perks. Make sure you are not also paying for those perks separately.

## 8. Software, domains and hosting

Antivirus, VPNs, password managers, website domains and hosting usually renew yearly, often at a higher price than the first year.

## 9. Other people's devices

Children's games, a partner's app or a parent's tablet may have subscriptions billed to your card or your family payment method.

## What to do once you find them

![Steps after finding subscriptions](fig:b)

Put everything in one list. RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, is built for this: add each subscription with its price, cycle and next date, and write where it is billed in the notes, such as "PayPal" or "carrier bill". That tells you exactly where to cancel later.

Cancel what you do not need, then mark it as cancelled in the app. Cancelled items move to their own list and do not count toward the free limit, so you keep a record of what you stopped.

## Stop new ones from hiding

From now on, add every new subscription and free trial on the day you start it. RenewRadar reminds you before each charge, so nothing gets the chance to hide again.
