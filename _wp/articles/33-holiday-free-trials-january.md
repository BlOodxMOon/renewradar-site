December is the busiest month for free trials. New phones and tablets come with offers, streaming services run holiday promotions, and fitness apps get downloaded for new year resolutions. All of those trials end in January.

## Why January is the danger month

- **Several trials end close together.** It is easy to lose track of which ends when.
- **Holiday spending is already high.** Unexpected charges are the last thing you need.
- **You are back at work and busy.** The trials you started on a relaxed afternoon end on a hectic weekday.
- **New devices bring bundled trials** that you may not even remember accepting.

![December trials ending in January](fig:a)

## Get ready in ten minutes

### 1. List every trial you started

Check Google Play's Subscriptions page, your email for "trial" and "welcome", and the setup screens of any new devices. Bundled trials for music, cloud storage and streaming are common with new phones.

### 2. Record the end date and price after each trial

Look for whether each trial becomes monthly or yearly. A yearly conversion can be a big January charge.

### 3. Put them all in one place

In RenewRadar, the subscription tracker app for Android, add each one with **Free trial** switched on. The Trials section at the top of Home lists them by end date, with days left and the price that starts afterwards.

![Holiday trials in RenewRadar](fig:b)

### 4. Set two reminders per trial

Three days and one day before the end gives you time to decide and a last chance to act. Reminders arrive on your phone even without the app open.

### 5. Cancel the clear no's now

If you already know you do not want to keep a trial, cancel it today. Most services, including subscriptions through Google Play, let you keep access until the trial ends.

## When the reminders arrive

For each trial, ask: did I use it in the last week? Would I pay the full price for it? If not, cancel and mark it as cancelled in RenewRadar. If yes, keep it, and RenewRadar tracks it as a regular subscription from then on.

## A calmer January

Holiday trials are a nice way to try new things at a relaxed time of year. With every end date in one list and a reminder before each one, January's statement only contains what you chose.
