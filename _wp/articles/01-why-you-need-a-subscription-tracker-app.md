Subscriptions are built around one simple idea: once you have said yes, you should not have to think about it again. That is convenient when you use the service every week. It is expensive when you stopped using it months ago, or when a free trial turned into a paid plan without you noticing.

A subscription tracker app fixes this by doing the remembering for you. It keeps a list of what you pay for, when each charge happens and how much it adds up to, and it reminds you before money leaves your account. RenewRadar is a subscription tracker and reminder app for Android that does exactly that, without a bank login or an account.

## Small numbers, big totals

A single subscription rarely looks expensive. A music plan, a cloud storage upgrade, a streaming service and one app you tried last spring might each cost less than a lunch. Together they can easily pass the cost of a phone bill, every month, every year.

The problem is not the price of any one service. It is that each charge arrives on a different day, from a different company, often in a different email inbox or app store. Nobody sees the total unless they add it up on purpose.

A subscription tracker app adds it up for you. In RenewRadar, weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly plans are turned into one monthly figure and one yearly figure, shown at the top of the Home screen. Seeing the yearly number is often the moment people decide what to keep.

## Free trials are where most surprises start

Free trials are a fair way to try a service. They also rely on a simple fact: many people forget to cancel in time. Some trials end after seven days, others after a month, and some switch straight to an annual plan when they end.

![A timeline from free trial sign-up to the first charge](fig:a)

If you start two or three trials around the same time, keeping track of their end dates in your head is unrealistic. A subscription reminder app gives every trial its own countdown, so you can decide on day five instead of discovering the charge on day eight.

## Your bank statement is too late

Bank and card statements show charges after they happen. By the time a forgotten renewal shows up, the money is gone, and getting it back depends on the company's refund policy.

A tracker works in the other direction. It looks ahead. RenewRadar reminds you before each renewal, with the timing you choose for each subscription: 14, 7, 3 or 1 day before, or on the day itself. That gives you time to cancel, downgrade or simply decide that you are happy to pay.

## A few subscriptions are exactly when it is easy

It is tempting to wait until your list is long before tracking it. The opposite works better. With three or four subscriptions, setting up a tracker takes about five minutes, and every new subscription after that takes about a minute to add.

RenewRadar is free for up to five subscriptions and free trials, which covers many people completely. Cancelled items do not count toward that limit, so you can keep a record of what you stopped paying for.

## What to look for in a subscription tracker app

Not every tracker works the same way. Before you install one, it is worth checking a few things:

- **How subscriptions get into the app.** Some apps read your bank transactions. Others, like RenewRadar, let you add them yourself, so the app never sees your bank account.
- **Whether free trials get special treatment.** Trials need a countdown and a reminder before they end, not after the first charge.
- **Reminder control.** A yearly plan deserves an earlier warning than a weekly one. Look for reminder timing per subscription.
- **Privacy.** Ask where your list is stored. RenewRadar keeps it on your phone and has no account to create.
- **Price.** A subscription tracker that is itself a subscription adds to the problem. RenewRadar Pro is a one-time payment.

## The short answer

You need a subscription tracker app for the same reason you set an alarm: not because you cannot remember, but because you have better things to remember. Even with a handful of subscriptions, one avoided charge usually covers the effort many times over.
