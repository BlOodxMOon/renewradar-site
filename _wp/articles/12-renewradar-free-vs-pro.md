RenewRadar has two versions: Free and Pro. Both are the same app, so you can start free and upgrade later without losing anything.

## What the free version includes

The free version is not a demo. It covers everything you need to avoid surprise charges, for up to five active subscriptions and free trials.

![Features included in the free version](fig:a)

A few details worth knowing:

- **Cancelled items do not count.** You can keep a record of what you stopped paying for without hitting the limit.
- **Trials count as items while active.** When you cancel one, it no longer counts.
- **There are no ads**, in the free version or in Pro.

## What Pro adds

Pro is for people who track more than five things or want to look further ahead.

![What Pro adds](fig:b)

- **Unlimited subscriptions and trials.**
- **Detailed reports**, including the charges expected over the next 12 months, so you can see which months will be expensive.
- **CSV export** of your full list, which you can open in a spreadsheet or keep as a backup when you change phones.

## How much Pro costs

Pro is a one-time purchase of US$9.99, shown in your local currency on Google Play. Prices can differ by country. It is not a subscription and never renews.

## How to decide

**Stay on Free if:**

- You pay for five or fewer subscriptions, including trials.
- You mostly want reminders and the monthly total.

**Choose Pro if:**

- You track more than five subscriptions and trials.
- You want to plan ahead with the 12-month view.
- You want a CSV backup of your list.
- You track work or business subscriptions and need an export for your records.

When you add a sixth active item, RenewRadar shows what Pro includes. You can close that screen and keep using the free version with your first five items.

## Buying and restoring Pro

Pro is bought through Google Play and linked to your Google account. If you reinstall the app or move to a new phone, open **Settings** and tap **Restore purchase**. You do not need an account with us.

Your subscription list is stored on your phone, not in the cloud. Restoring Pro brings back the features, not the list, so export a CSV before you switch phones.

## The short version

Most people with a handful of subscriptions can use RenewRadar free forever. If your list is longer, Pro costs less than many single subscriptions cost in a year, and you pay for it once.
