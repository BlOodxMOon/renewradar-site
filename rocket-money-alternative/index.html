The main difference

Rocket Money finds subscriptions by reading the transactions in bank accounts you connect, and adds budgeting, net worth and other money tools on top. That needs a US bank and an account with Rocket Money.

RenewRadar does one job: it tracks the subscriptions and free trials you add, shows what they cost, and reminds you before each charge. It has no account, does not connect to banks, works in any country and currency, and stores everything on your phone.

When Rocket Money is the better fit

  • You live in the US and want subscriptions found automatically from your bank.
  • You want a full budgeting app with spending categories, savings and net worth.
  • You want someone else to handle cancellations and bill negotiation.

When RenewRadar is the better fit

  • You live outside the US, or your bank is not supported by bank-linking services.
  • You do not want to link your bank or create another online account.
  • You pay in more than one currency. Each subscription keeps its own currency.
  • Free trials are your main worry. Trials get their own countdown and reminders.
  • You do not want another subscription to track your subscriptions. Pro is a one-time purchase.

Switching over

If you already use a bank-linked app, its subscription list is a good starting point. Open it, add each recurring charge to RenewRadar with its next charge date, and pick your reminder timing. Five subscriptions take about five minutes. Once you are done, you can disconnect your bank from the old app if you no longer need it.
